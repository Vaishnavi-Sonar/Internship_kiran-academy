Section B — Generative AI & LLM Concepts
11. LLM: Large Language Model.
12. Generative AI: AI that creates new content, such as text, images, audio, or video.
13. Prompt: Instructions or input given to an AI model.
14. System prompt: Instructions that define an AI model’s behavior and role.
15. AI hallucination: When AI generates incorrect or fabricated information as if it were true.
16. Token: A unit of text or data processed by an AI model.
17. Context window: The amount of information a model can consider in one interaction.
18. Inference: The process of using a trained model to generate predictions or responses.
19. Fine-tuning: Further training a pretrained model on specific data to adapt its behavior.
20. Multimodal AI: AI that can process or generate multiple types of data, such as text, images, audio, and video.
Section C — Agents, RAG & Modern AI Development
21. AI agent: An AI system that can pursue a goal, make decisions, and use tools to perform tasks.
22. Difference: A chatbot mainly responds to messages, while an AI agent can take actions and complete multi-step tasks.
23. RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
24. Purpose of RAG: To retrieve relevant external information and use it to generate more grounded answers.
25. Embedding: A numerical vector representing the meaning or features of data.
26. Vector database: Stores and searches vector embeddings for similarity-based retrieval.
27. Tool calling: Allows an AI model to request actions from external tools, functions, or APIs.
28. MCP: Model Context Protocol.
29. API key: A credential used to authenticate and authorize access to an API.
30. Agentic workflow: A multi-step process where an AI system plans, uses tools, and acts toward a goal.
